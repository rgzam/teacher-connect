# Product notes from Ly

These came from a real conversation. The booking form follows them.

## Required on the parent booking page

- Student first name and last name
- Parent first name and last name
- Virtual meeting name, when the meeting is virtual

## Optional

- Parent email
- Parent phone
- Home address on a home visit (the parent can share it later)

## Two meeting types

- **Virtual** — video meeting. Meeting name is required so Ly knows what to look for on the call.
- **Home visit** — Ly goes to the family. A coworker goes with her.

## Reminders

About one hour before a confirmed meeting, email Ly and the parent (if they left an email).

- Virtual: remind both
- Home visit: remind both, and the teacher email says to bring a coworker
